
Controls: Press Enter to launch. Move with WASD or the arrow keys and hold Space to fire. Enemy
ships and their projectiles drain shields. When all four shields are gone, press R to
retry.


five ships: The five starter-kit ships cycle through a straight descent, a wide weave, a tracking
dive, a fast zigzag, and a heavy patrol. The last three types fire aimed shots. The
heavy ship takes four hits.
